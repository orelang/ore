# ORE5041

> Field '…' of type '…' must be initialized in its declaration or at the top level of the constructor.

Group: [Classes and interfaces](README.md#ore5xxx-classes-and-interfaces) · specification: [03-objects.md](../spec/03-objects.md)

## Example

```ts
    private secret = 1
    readonly id: int = 0
    next: Point
```

`tests/diag/types/classes.ore:4`: Field 'next' of type 'Point' must be initialized in its declaration or at the top level of the constructor.
